## P0 findings — all closed

### SRC-003 — deleted announcement could stay actionable
`DEEP_DESIGN.md:411` retained evidence from a disappeared source and refused to infer revocation,
which is correct. But `DEEP_DESIGN.md:450` let one *current* T1/T2 source make a date actionable, and
nothing defined whether `unavailable` evidence still counts as `current`. Under the weaker reading, a
deleted allowlist post could still drive an URGENT alert and a CTA.

**PATCH:** `unavailable` evidence is explicitly not `current` evidence for `ACTION`/`URGENT`, stage
transitions, or any wallet-impacting CTA. It degrades to WATCH with the disappearance shown. This
closes the hole without inferring revocation.
**Fixture:** `F29`.

### SAFETY-MANIP-001 — compromised official account could manufacture urgency
The CTA gate is strong against hostile *domains*, and conflict detection handles contradicting
sources. Neither covers **silence**: a compromised official account posting a false deadline with the
project's own genuine URL produces no new host, no contract change, and no conflict. A single T1/T2
source then made the date actionable and could reach URGENT.

**PATCH:** a newly appeared or shortened deadline, or a transition to `OPEN`, resting on a single
account-based official source cannot escalate to `URGENT` or carry a CTA. Escalation needs a second
independent official surface, on-chain evidence, or the claim present on the canonical surface.
Single-source claims still alert at WATCH/ACTION, so the product's lead time is preserved.

The reviewer's blunter recommendation — require corroboration for *all* deadline claims — was
**rejected**: it would have removed the early single-source detection this product exists to provide.
The patch separates discovery from urgency escalation instead.
**Fixture:** `F30`. **Schema:** `DecisionOutput.urgency_corroboration`.

### RSC-001 — opportunity cancellation was unrepresentable
`MintStage.state` is a proper enum including `CANCELLED`, but `Opportunity.state` was `string`
(`DEEP_DESIGN.md:225`) and the canonical flow at `:490` had no `CANCELLED`, while `F18` already
expected `opportunity -> CANCELLED`. An implementation would either reject the transition as illegal
or invent an uncontracted value, leaving a stale actionable opportunity sending mint reminders.

**PATCH:** `Opportunity.state` is now a finite enum including `CANCELLED` and `EXPIRED`, and both are
always-legal targets from any pre-`ENDED` / actionable state. Entering `CANCELLED` revokes the CTA and
stops reminders.
**Fixture:** `F31`. **Schema:** opportunity state hard rules in `HARNESS_SCHEMAS.md`.

### MA-GOV-001 — the gate could authorize coding without this Red Team
Section D's boxes were checked for the design-time blind-spot sweep, while `PROJECT_STATUS.md` said
the cross-system Red Team had not run. Section H said "only after the remaining E + G items", so a
maintainer could legitimately reach `PHASE_1_CODING_READY` without it.

This contradiction was introduced by the same-day edit that moved the gate to `FREEZE_PENDING`.

**PATCH:** section D is relabelled a preliminary design-time sweep, the cross-system Red Team is a
separate explicit checklist, and section H now requires unresolved P0 = 0 as a third condition.

## Downgraded on review

### SAFETY-MANIP-002 — P0 claimed, accepted as P1
Claim: `official_action_url: VerifiedLink` invites an implementation to render an action URL without
the CTA assessment. Verified real as a schema trap. Downgraded because the guarantee does exist in
canonical text — `DEEP_DESIGN.md:763`, "only CONSISTENT may render as wallet-impacting CTA" — so this
is implementation-drift risk, not an unguarded design.

Patched anyway, because this project's own rule puts hard guarantees in schema rather than prose:
`official_action_url` is declared non-renderable source evidence, and `DecisionOutput.cta_link` now
carries an `ActionLinkAssessment` instead of a bare `VerifiedLink`.

re-read every cited line. Two reviewer conclusions did not survive that check unchanged: one severity
was lowered, and one recommendation was replaced because it would have damaged the product's core
value. Reviewer output was treated as evidence to verify, not as a verdict to apply.
